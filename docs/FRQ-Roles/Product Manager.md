Product Managers

**PM01. Feature Readiness Reviewer**

**Purpose:** Check whether a feature has sufficient clarity to enter ART planning.

**Checks:**

- Business and operational value.

- Target users or stakeholders.

- Benefit hypothesis.

- measurable outcome.

- acceptance conditions.

- solution and delivery dependencies.

- security/safety/regulatory relevance.

- affected products or components.

- required enablers.

- linked source decisions.

**PM02. Feature-to-Story Alignment Checker**

**Purpose:** Verify whether team stories collectively support the feature intent.

**Identifies:**

- Feature outcomes without supporting stories.

- Stories not traceable to a feature.

- Duplicated scope.

- Missing integration work.

- Missing testing, documentation, or operational work.

- Stories that implement activity but not the intended outcome.

**PM03. Roadmap Dependency Analyzer**

**Purpose:** Analyse dependencies among features, teams, releases, components, and external decisions.

**Outputs:**

- dependency chain,

- critical assumptions,

- sequencing options,

- unresolved ownership,

- evidence gaps,

- potential integration risk.

**PM04. WSJF Preparation Assistant**

**Purpose:** Prepare inputs for a human WSJF conversation.

**What it may do:**

- Gather existing value, urgency, risk-reduction, opportunity-enablement, and size inputs.

- Identify missing evidence.

- explain disagreements between sources.

- produce scenarios.

**What it must not do:** Invent scores or automatically reorder the backlog

**PM05. PI Objective Quality Reviewer**

**Purpose:** Review PI objectives for business clarity and measurability.

**Checks:**

- Outcome rather than activity.

- Clear business meaning.

- Verifiable success condition.

- Dependencies and constraints.

- Link to approved strategic or product context.

- Committed versus uncommitted status, when explicitly available.

**PM06. Product Decision Brief Generator**

**Purpose:** Prepare an evidence-based decision brief.

**Structure:**

- Decision required.

- Context.

- Options explicitly considered.

- Benefits.

- risks.

- dependencies.

- compliance/safety implications.

- evidence.

- assumptions.

- recommendation.

- approver.

**PM07. Change Impact Across Product Analyzer**

**Purpose:** Assess how a proposed change may affect products, teams, releases, requirements, integrations, tests, support, and documentation.

**Special Frequentis value:** It can help connect individual team changes to MosaiX-wide integration consequences.

**PM08. Product Risk and Compliance Intake**

**Purpose:** Identify whether a planned feature requires early involvement from security, safety, architecture, quality, legal, privacy, or regulatory stakeholders.

**Safe behavior:** Routes questions to appropriate review. It does not issue a compliance determination.

**PM09. Customer and Stakeholder Insight Synthesizer**

**Purpose:** Consolidate approved findings from interviews, feedback, support data, and product discussions.

**Output:**

- recurring needs,

- observed pain points,

- conflicting expectations,

- source evidence,

- unanswered questions,

- candidate opportunities.

**Safe rule:** No emotional or personality inference about stakeholders.

**PM10. Release Scope Evidence Reviewer**

**Purpose:** Review whether the proposed release scope is supported by readiness evidence.

**Checks:**

- Feature and story readiness.

- dependency status.

- integration evidence.

- quality evidence.

- documentation.

- known deviations.

- unresolved risks.

- decision ownership.

**PM11. Product Outcome Measurement Designer**

**Purpose:** Propose meaningful product and flow measures without overstating causality.

**Measure categories:**

- Customer or operational outcome.

- Delivery flow.

- Product quality.

- Adoption.

- Support impact.

- Risk.

- evidence maturity.

Internal Frequentis material already cautions that predictability, carry-over, story completion, and estimation indicators are influenced by incidents, Problem Reports, story granularity, and estimation practices. They should be contextual indicators, not promised AI outcomes.

**PM12. ART-Level Progress and Integration View**

**Purpose:** Consolidate feature progress and integration readiness across teams.

**Produces:**

- feature-level status based on evidence,

- contributing team work,

- dependencies,

- integration readiness,

- unresolved decisions,

- risk and confidence indicators,

- areas where evidence is incomplete.
