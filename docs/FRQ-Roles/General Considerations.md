**6. Recommended cross-role Skills**

These Skills should be available to more than one role, with role-specific outputs.

| **Skill** | **Product Owner** | **Scrum Master** | **Product Manager** |
|----|----|----|----|
| Requirement ambiguity detection | Primary | View | Oversight |
| Story/feature readiness | Primary at story level | Process check | Primary at feature level |
| Traceability analysis | Primary | Flow visibility | Product/ART oversight |
| Dependency radar | Team dependencies | Primary facilitator | Cross-team ownership |
| Progress evidence | Story evidence | Sprint flow | Feature/ART integration |
| Decision recorder | Functional decisions | Process decisions | Product decisions |
| Risk intake | Story risk | Delivery risk | Product/ART risk |
| Documentation synchronization | Story/document link | Done-flow check | Release/product check |
| KPI interpretation | Value evidence | Flow metrics | Product outcomes |
| AI-use safety check | Required | Required | Required |
